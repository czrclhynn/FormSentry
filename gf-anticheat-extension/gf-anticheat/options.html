    Settings & privacy

    Monitoring rules and domains belong to each examination. A started attempt keeps its original rules. Use a practice exam before sharing a live invitation.

    Students can stop monitoring for technical problems or withdraw consent. Google Forms submission routes are observations; only a separate response integration could independently verify acceptance. This release does not claim that integration.

    Records are stored in your institution’s Supabase project. Protect invitation links, limit report sharing, and apply your institution’s retention policy. This extension cannot identify external applications or monitor other devices or browser profiles.
